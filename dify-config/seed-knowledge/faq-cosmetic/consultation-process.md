# Cosmetic Consultation Process

Question: What is the general consultation process for cosmetic treatment?

Answer:
1. The patient shares their goals, preferred procedure, country, and rough budget.
2. The team reviews whether the request is suitable for an initial consultation.
3. If more context is needed, the chatbot or coordinator asks for photos, medical history, or previous treatment details.
4. A coordinator follows up to confirm contact details and explain the next step.
5. If the patient wants to move forward, the conversation can transition into case creation.

Notes:
- The first consultation is for information gathering and expectation alignment.
- Final treatment recommendations depend on case review and provider assessment.
