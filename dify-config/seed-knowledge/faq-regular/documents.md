# Regular Medical Consultation Documents

Question: What documents are usually needed before a regular medical consultation?

Answer:
Patients are commonly asked to provide:
- recent medical reports
- imaging or scans if available
- pathology or lab results if relevant
- a short symptom summary
- previous treatment history
- contact details, country, and approximate budget

Notes:
- The exact list depends on the specialty and condition.
- If a patient already has records ready, the chatbot can guide them to upload the files immediately.
