# Regular Medical Consultation Process

Question: What is the general consultation process for regular medical treatment?

Answer:
1. The patient describes their symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment goal.
2. The team checks whether enough information is available for an initial review.
3. If records are missing, the patient is asked to upload reports, scans, and a short symptom summary.
4. A coordinator or case team reviews the information and explains the next step.
5. If the patient wants to proceed, the conversation can move into case creation and provider matching.

Notes:
- Medical cases usually require more supporting records than simple FAQ requests.
- The first step is to collect enough information for safe review, not to promise treatment.
