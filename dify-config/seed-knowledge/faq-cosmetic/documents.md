# Cosmetic Consultation Documents

Question: What documents or materials are usually needed before a cosmetic consultation?

Answer:
Patients are usually asked to provide:
- clear recent photos of the treatment area
- a short summary of their goals or concerns
- any prior surgery or treatment history related to the area
- relevant medical reports if they had complications or revision history
- contact details, country, and approximate budget

Notes:
- The exact list depends on the procedure.
- If the patient already has reports or images, the chatbot can guide them to upload files first.
