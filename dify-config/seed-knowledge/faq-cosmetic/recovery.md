# Cosmetic Recovery Basics

Question: How long is the recovery period for common cosmetic procedures?

Answer:
Recovery varies by procedure, but a basic expectation is:
- mild swelling or bruising may last several days to two weeks
- patients often need a short downtime period before travel or social activities
- revision or more complex procedures may require a longer recovery window

Notes:
- Exact recovery time depends on the procedure, the provider's protocol, and the patient's condition.
- The chatbot should avoid giving a guaranteed timeline for a specific patient without case review.
